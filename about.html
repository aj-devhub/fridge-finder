About Fridge Finder

Fridge Finder was built to solve a simple problem: figuring out what to cook with the random ingredients you already have at home.

By using the Spoonacular API, this app helps you reduce food waste, save money, and discover new recipes without having to run to the grocery store.

Created by Arowolaje Joshua Oluwatimileyin for WDD 330.
